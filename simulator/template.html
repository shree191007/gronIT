What this model is, and is not

  • Flow panel: a 2D lattice-Boltzmann slice at Re ≈ 156. The real pod sees Re ≈ 4×10⁵ and a turbulent wake, so the vortex street here is a laminar stand-in.
  • Heat transfer: hull U of 200 to 800 W/m²K, 8 MJ/K loop thermal mass, a 38 K chip-to-coolant rise at full power and the thermostat bypass are assumptions. Vendor specs for the actual racks govern.
  • Bandwidth: the 130 TB/s rack NVLink figure is the NVL72 spec. The 3.2 Tb/s umbilical, 1 Gb/s satellite link and 4 KB per request are my assumptions.
  • Ocean column: depth, spacing and the plume glow are schematic. The real bulk warming is a few hundredths of a kelvin.

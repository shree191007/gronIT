Antarctic Neocloud

Place subsea compute nodes where they disturb the least ecology. Every node sends its waste heat ashore to a station, so nothing is rejected into the water.

Layer
Hover the map to read a site. Click to inspect it and place a node by hand.

Sensitivity layers are hand-placed from published distributions of krill, colonies, foraging areas and shelf habitat. Coastlines, protected-area boundaries and station heat demand are simplified. Treat the output as a siting method for the pitch, then load CCAMLR and SCAR data before quoting figures. Cost inputs are editable placeholders.
